Integrity matter

Censure by the New South Wales Legislative Council over his response to a staff member's alleged vandalism of a Captain Cook statue and his attendance at a forest protest (2020)

David Shoebridge · Greens · 16 June 2020

Official finding

Description

In June 2020, while Mr Shoebridge was a member of the New South Wales Legislative Council, a part-time member of his parliamentary staff was charged over the alleged spray-painting of a statue of Captain Cook in Hyde Park, Sydney. On 16 June 2020 the Legislative Council passed a government censure motion, supported by Labor and One Nation members, that condemned Mr Shoebridge for what it described as his failure to publicly address community concerns about his staff member's alleged involvement in defacing a public monument, and for travelling to a protest against logging in Nambucca State Forest after attending a Black Lives Matter rally in Sydney during the COVID-19 restrictions. Mr Shoebridge told the chamber he would not dismiss the staff member, whose alleged conduct occurred outside work hours, and said he attended the forest protest at the request of Gumbaynggirr elders. The criminal matter concerned the staff member, not Mr Shoebridge.
