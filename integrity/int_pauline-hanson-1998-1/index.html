Integrity matter

Queensland prosecution over the 1997 registration of Pauline Hanson's One Nation: convictions quashed and acquittals entered

Pauline Hanson · One Nation · 10 July 1998

Overturned / appealed

Outcome: Overturned / appealed. An earlier finding was overturned on appeal, or is under appeal (see outcome).

Description

In October 1997 Ms Hanson applied to register Pauline Hanson's One Nation as a Queensland political party on the basis that it had more than 500 members. After a civil challenge, the Supreme Court of Queensland set the registration aside in August 1999, finding the Electoral Commissioner's decision had been induced by fraud or misrepresentation. Following a police investigation, Ms Hanson and David Ettridge were charged in July 2001 with dishonestly inducing the registration, and Ms Hanson was also charged with dishonestly obtaining electoral reimbursements. A District Court jury convicted both on 20 August 2003 and each was sentenced to three years' imprisonment. On 6 November 2003 the Queensland Court of Appeal quashed the convictions, set aside the sentences and directed that verdicts of acquittal be entered. A Crime and Misconduct Commission inquiry requested by the Queensland Parliament found in January 2004 that no misconduct or impropriety was associated with the litigation or the police investigation.

Outcome

Convictions quashed; verdicts of acquittal entered (Queensland Court of Appeal, 6 November 2003). No conviction stands. The Crime and Misconduct Commission (January 2004) found that 'no misconduct or other impropriety has been shown to have been associated with the conduct of the litigation concerning Ms Hanson and Mr Ettridge, or with the police investigations leading to the prosecution'.
