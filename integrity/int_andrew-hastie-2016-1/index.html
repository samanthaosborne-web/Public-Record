Integrity matter

Termination of Mr Hastie's Standby Army Reserve service after he declined a Defence request to remove images of himself in uniform from 2016 election material

Andrew Hastie · Liberal · 9 June 2016

Official finding

Description

During the 2016 federal election campaign, Defence asked Mr Hastie, then the Member for Canning and a member of the Standby Army Reserve, to remove images of himself in uniform from campaign material, citing policy that Australian Defence Force members not engage in political activity in uniform without approval. Mr Hastie declined, saying the image reflected his personal and professional history and that as a former officer he was no longer under military authority. The Army issued a notice of intention to terminate his service for failing to comply with directions and Defence policy, and his reserve service was terminated.
