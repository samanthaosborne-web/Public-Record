Referral of Regional Connectivity Grants round 3 to the National Integrity Commission

Eleanor Vance · Harbour Alliance · 18 February 2026

Formal investigation

Not a finding. A formal investigation is under way. This is not a finding. An allegation, referral or investigation is not evidence of wrongdoing.

Description

The Auditor-General reported on 10 December 2025 that 61 per cent of round-3 grant funding went to electorates held by the government and that the minister's office approved 14 grants against departmental advice. An opposition member referred the program to the National Integrity Commission on 18 February 2026. The commission announced a preliminary assessment on 4 March 2026 and a formal investigation on 22 June 2026.
