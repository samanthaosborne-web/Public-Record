Use of electorate office printing for a private business mailing

Tom Brackenridge · Civic Reform · 15 January 2026

Official finding

Description

A newspaper reported on 15 January 2026 that Mr Brackenridge's electorate office printing allocation had been used for a flyer promoting a private business. The authority opened an investigation on 5 February 2026. On 27 March 2026 it found a minor breach, required repayment of $1,240 and found no dishonest intent. The amount was repaid on 2 April 2026.
