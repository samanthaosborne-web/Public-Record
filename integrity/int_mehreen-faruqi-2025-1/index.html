Integrity matter

Senate resolution on the conduct of senators after Senator Faruqi's sign protest during the Governor-General's address opening the 48th Parliament

Mehreen Faruqi · Greens · 22 July 2025

Official finding

Description

On 22 July 2025, during the Governor-General's address at the opening of the 48th Parliament, Senator Faruqi held up a sign referring to Gaza and calling for sanctions on Israel, and as the Prime Minister left the chamber asked whether he would sanction Israel. On 23 July 2025 the Senate agreed to a motion on the conduct of senators moved by Senator Wong, and the President made a statement about conduct during the address. Guardian Australia reported that the resolution described the protest as 'utterly disrespectful' and excluded Senator Faruqi from overseas parliamentary delegations for the remainder of the 48th Parliament, and that a Coalition amendment that would also have suspended her from the Senate for the rest of the week was unsuccessful. This was a resolution of the Senate, not a matter referred to the Privileges Committee.
