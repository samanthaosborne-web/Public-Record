Integrity matter

Electoral Commission review of the party's 2019 election funding claim: overpayment and enforceable undertaking given as party agent

Pauline Hanson · One Nation · 4 March 2021

Official finding

Description

As registered agent for Pauline Hanson's One Nation, Senator Hanson lodged the party's final election funding claim for the 2019 federal election. On review, the AEC found the claim deficient because a number of expenditure items could not be substantiated, being either not electoral expenditure or expenditure that had not been incurred, resulting in an overpayment of $165,442.03. On 4 March 2021 the Electoral Commissioner varied the party's entitlement to $2,675,324.42. Senator Hanson then gave a written undertaking, accepted by the Electoral Commissioner on 15 June 2021 under the Regulatory Powers (Standard Provisions) Act 2014, that future claims would specify all electoral expenditure with invoices matched to evidence of payment. The undertaking was given in her capacity as party agent.
