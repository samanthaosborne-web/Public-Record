Claim

“That is the Uluru Statement from the Heart on an A4 bit of paper. That is it.”

Speaker
Anthony Albanese
Labor
Date
8 August 2023
Location / context
House of Representatives, Question Time, responding to claims that the statement was 26 pages long

Evidence status

Supported

The best available evidence is consistent with the claim as stated.

What the evidence shows

The National Indigenous Australians Agency told CheckMate that the Uluru Statement from the Heart is a one-page document, as confirmed by its authors. The 26-page document cited by critics was one of several documents released on the agency's freedom-of-information disclosure log in March 2023; it contains the one-page statement together with background material from the Referendum Council's regional dialogues, which the council's 2017 final report also reproduces.

Context

The statement was made after a Sky News presenter and the Shadow Minister for Indigenous Australians said a 26-page version of the Uluru Statement had been 'exposed' through freedom of information. The dispute was part of the 2023 Voice referendum campaign. CheckMate did not use a one-word verdict label; its finding is quoted as the verdict.
