Claim

“Scientific records show that bleaching has been a natural part of the Great Barrier Reef's cycles and other reef cycles for millennia ... That is fact.”

Speaker
Malcolm Roberts
One Nation
Date
March 2024
Location / context
Senate speech on a motion about the Great Barrier Reef (March 2024; published on the senator's website and as a video on X on 25 March 2024)

Evidence status

Contradicted by evidence

The best available evidence is inconsistent with the claim as stated.

What the evidence shows

Australian Institute of Marine Science records show mass bleaching on the reef was first documented with full-scale surveys in 1998, followed by 2002, 2016, 2017, 2020 and 2022, with the 2024 event the fifth in eight years. A University of Queensland coral scientist told AAP FactCheck that cores from corals hundreds of years old show no earlier mass bleaching events, and a co-author of the University of Sydney study cited in the speech said it examined reef growth over 20,000 to 30,000-year cycles and did not look at bleaching.
