Claim

“That's 790,000 new jobs created. More than any first term government on record.”

Speaker
Anthony Albanese
Labor
Date
26 March 2024
Location / context
Post on X (video)

Evidence status

Mixed / context required

The claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

What the evidence shows

ABS labour force data show there were 789,700 more employed people in February 2024 than in May 2022, the largest such increase for a first-term government since 1966, the earliest year for which broadly comparable data exist. Labour economists told RMIT ABC Fact Check that raw numbers ignore population growth: adjusted for population, the increase in employment during the first 21 months of the Hawke government in 1983-84 was larger, and that government also fared better on hours worked, underemployment and the share of new jobs that were full-time. Experts also said governments cannot take sole credit for job creation, most of which occurs in the private sector.
