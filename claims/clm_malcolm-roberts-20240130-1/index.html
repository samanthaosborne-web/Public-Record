Claim

“...And this doesn't include the 2.7 million visa holders in Australia...”

Speaker
Malcolm Roberts
One Nation
Date
30 January 2024
Location / context
Post on X, sharing an ABC News post about the population reaching 27 million

Evidence status

Contradicted by evidence

The best available evidence is inconsistent with the claim as stated.

What the evidence shows

The ABS told AAP FactCheck that its estimated resident population, which underlies the population clock that passed 27 million on 24 January 2024, counts anyone in Australia for 12 of 16 months regardless of visa type. Most of the 2.76 million temporary visa holders recorded by the Department of Home Affairs at 31 December 2023, including 701,868 New Zealanders on Special Category visas and 547,075 student visa holders, are therefore already included; only the 601,051 visitor-visa holders would mostly be excluded.

Most of the 2.76 million temporary visa holders recorded by the Department of Home Affairs at 31 December 2023, including 701,868 New Zealanders on Special Category visas and 547,075 student visa holders, are therefore already included; only the 601,051 visitor-visa holders would mostly be excluded.

Primary evidence

  • Tier 1 · PrimaryDataset
    Population clock and pyramidabs.gov.au

    Australian Bureau of Statistics — The clock projects the estimated resident population; it passed 27 million on 24 January 2024.

  • Tier 1 · PrimaryDataset
    Technical note: the 12/16 month rule for measuring overseas migrationabs.gov.au

    Australian Bureau of Statistics — A person is counted in the resident population if in Australia for 12 months or more over a 16-month period, irrespective of visa.

  • Tier 1 · PrimaryDataset

    Department of Home Affairs via data.gov.au — 2.76 million temporary visa holders, including 701,868 Special Category, 547,075 student and 601,051 visitor visa holders.

Context
