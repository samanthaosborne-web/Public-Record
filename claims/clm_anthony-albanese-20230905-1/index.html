Claim

“In between I will have the first bilateral visit by an Australian leader to the Philippines in over two decades”

Speaker
Anthony Albanese
Labor
Date
5 September 2023
Location / context
House of Representatives, discussing the forthcoming visit to Indonesia, the Philippines and India

Evidence status

Mostly supported

The evidence supports the substance of the claim with minor inaccuracies or omissions.

What the evidence shows

Former prime minister Malcolm Turnbull held bilateral meetings with Philippine presidents in the Philippines in November 2015 and November 2017, but on both occasions he was in the country for multilateral summits (APEC in 2015 and ASEAN in 2017). The Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet told AAP FactCheck it defines a bilateral visit as one 'not conducted in the margins of a regional or multilateral summit or conference'. On that definition the claim holds; one international relations expert said that in practical terms a meeting on the sidelines of a summit is 'the same thing'.
