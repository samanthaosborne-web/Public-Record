Parliamentary career

Elected to the House of Representatives for Hume, New South Wales, at the 2013 election and re-elected in 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2025. The Parliament of Australia records his Liberal Party service from 7 September 2013. He held assistant-ministerial and ministerial office from February 2016 to May 2022, served on the Opposition frontbench from June 2022, and is recorded as Leader of the Opposition and Leader of the Federal Parliamentary Liberal Party from 13 February 2026. He has no Senate or state parliamentary service.

Evidence status

Contradicted by evidenceThe best available evidence is inconsistent with the claim as stated.

NAB's 17 August 2026 economic newsletter described an illustrative scenario: if rental yields rose by one percentage point and property prices stayed flat, Sydney and Melbourne rents would need to rise by 25 to 30 per cent. It said any adjustment was more likely to combine lower dwelling prices and higher rents. In a note issued on 18 August, before the question was asked, NAB stated that the figure 'was not a forecast'. Ray White, whose modelling produced a similar figure, said its scenario assumed no price falls, which it considered unlikely, and was 'not a forecast of rent growth'. No published NAB material forecast a 30 per cent rise in rents.

    AAP FactCheck — Verdict as published: Misleading.

Evidence status

Mixed / context requiredThe claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

Mr Taylor's office said the figure compared the ABS net overseas migration estimate for the year to September 2025 (311,000) with the forecast of 225,000 for 2026-27 in the December 2024 Mid-Year Economic and Fiscal Outlook, a gap of 86,000. The 2026-27 financial year had not begun. Compared with the forecast for the year in progress, 2025-26, of 260,000 in the December 2025 MYEFO, the overshoot was about 51,000. The May 2026 budget later raised the forecasts to 295,000 for 2025-26 and 245,000 for 2026-27. Experts consulted by AAP FactCheck said the like-for-like comparison was with the 2025-26 forecast, while noting that forecasts have routinely undershot actual migration since the pandemic under both governments.

“They [Ampol] are being forced by Labor's emissions standards to export their fuel right now”

19 March 2026 · Sky News interview clip posted by Mr Taylor on Facebook

Original source

  • Tier 1 · PrimaryTranscript· 19 March 2026
    Sky News interview clip posted to Facebook, 19 March 2026facebook.com

    Angus Taylor (Facebook)

Evidence status

Mixed / context requiredThe claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

A Parliamentary Library chronology shows that the Morrison government's Fuel Quality Standards (Petrol) Determination 2019 set a 10 parts-per-million sulphur limit from 1 July 2027, and that in 2021-22 the Coalition paid Ampol and Viva Energy $137.5 million each to meet the lower limit by the end of 2024 without legislating that date. The Albanese government legislated a 2024 deadline in 2022, then moved it to December 2025. Ampol told the ASX in October 2025 that its refinery upgrade would not be finished before mid-2026, and it exported higher-sulphur petrol in the meantime. After the standards were temporarily relaxed from 12 March 2026, Ampol said it could supply an extra 80 to 100 million litres a month domestically. The exports were connected to the compliance deadline the current government set, but the standard itself originated under the Coalition.
