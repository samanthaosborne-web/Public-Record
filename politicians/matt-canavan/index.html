Parliamentary career

Elected to the Senate for Queensland at the 2013 election and re-elected in 2016 and 2022. The Parliament of Australia records no state parliamentary service and no casual-vacancy appointment. He was a cabinet minister in the Turnbull and Morrison governments from February 2016, standing aside from the ministry on 25 July 2017 during the section 44 citizenship reference and returning on 27 October 2017, the day the High Court ruled he was validly elected; he left the ministry on 3 February 2020. He served as Nationals Whip in the Senate in 2015-16 and 2025-26 and as Deputy Leader of The Nationals in the Senate from 2019 to 2022, and is recorded as Leader of the Federal Parliamentary Nationals from 11 March 2026, with a shadow cabinet portfolio from 16 March 2026.

Evidence status

Mixed / context requiredThe claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

The CSIRO's GenCost 2024-25 final report gives a 2024 levelised cost range of $111 to $178 per megawatt hour for black coal and $120 to $168 for solar and wind with firming, with near-identical midpoints ($144.50 and $144). For 2030 the ranges are $103 to $174 for coal (midpoint $138.50) and $76 to $116 for firmed renewables (midpoint $96). The post used only the low end of the 2024 coal range and omitted the 2030 part of the chart. GenCost's lead author and a CSIRO spokesperson told AAP FactCheck that midpoints are the reasonable comparison and that renewables remain the lowest-cost new-build generation technology.

Original statement

“First day without the Liddell coal power station and the notices of a lack of power are flying thick and fast. Not a good sign and it is not even winter yet.”

28 April 2023 · Post on X, with screenshots of four Australian Energy Market Operator market notices

Original source

  • Tier 1 · PrimaryTranscript· 28 April 2023
    Post on X, 28 April 2023twitter.com

    Matt Canavan (X)

Evidence status

Mixed / context requiredThe claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

Four AEMO market notices were issued on the day Liddell's final unit was powered down, as the post showed; three were subsequently cancelled. AEMO's own explainer says a 'lack of reserve level 1' forecast 'simply provides an indication to the market to encourage more generation' and has 'no impact to power system security or reliability'. AEMO's quarterly reports show such notices are routine: 19 in the March 2023 quarter, 45 in the December 2022 quarter and 89 in the September 2022 quarter. Energy analysts Alison Reeve (Grattan Institute) and Bruce Mountain (Victoria University) said neither the New South Wales notices nor a South Australian intervention notice could be attributed to Liddell's closure.

Context

The notices were real, but the fact-check found no evidence linking them to the closure of Liddell, and the type of notice involved is a routine market signal rather than a warning of supply failure. PUBLIC RECORD classifies the claim as mixed because the notices existed as described while the implied connection to the closure was not supported.
