Parliamentary career

The Parliament of Australia records state service as a Member of the Tasmanian House of Assembly for Franklin from the 2002 election until his resignation on 2 August 2015, including as Leader of the Tasmanian Greens from 7 July 2008 to 2014 and as a Tasmanian minister from April 2010 in portfolios including Human Services and Community Development and Sustainable Transport. Federally, he was chosen by the Parliament of Tasmania on 19 August 2015, under section 15 of the Constitution, to fill the Senate casual vacancy caused by the resignation of Senator Christine Milne; he was elected to the Senate for Tasmania at the 2016 election and re-elected in 2019 and 2025. His Australian Greens service is recorded from 19 August 2015.
