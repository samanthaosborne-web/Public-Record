Original statement

“Forty per cent of the nation's food comes out of Farrer, so it's pretty important.”

23 August 2026 · Interview, ABC TV Insiders

Original source

  • Tier 2 · IndependentTranscript· 23 August 2026

    ABC News (YouTube)

Evidence status

Contradicted by evidenceThe best available evidence is inconsistent with the claim as stated.

Using ABS 2020–21 value-of-production data for the 48 statistical areas wholly or partly within Farrer, an analysis by Flinders University water economist Professor Sarah Wheeler for AAP FactCheck estimated the electorate's agricultural output at about $4.4 billion of a $71 billion national total, around 6 per cent. The 40 per cent figure matches the whole Murray-Darling Basin, which the Murray-Darling Basin Authority says produces around 40 per cent of Australia's agricultural output and which is roughly eight times the size of the electorate.

Context

In a Facebook video on 28 June 2026 Mr Farley applied the 40 per cent figure to the Southern Murray-Darling Basin, an area about four times larger than Farrer.
