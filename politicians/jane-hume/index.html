Parliamentary career

Elected to the Senate for Victoria at the 2016 election and re-elected in 2019 and 2025. The Parliament of Australia records her Liberal Party service from 2 July 2016. She served as Temporary Chair of Committees and Government Deputy Whip from September 2018 to May 2019, held assistant-ministerial and ministerial office from May 2019 to May 2022, served in the Shadow Cabinet from June 2022 to May 2025, and is recorded as Deputy Leader of the Opposition and Deputy Leader of the Federal Parliamentary Liberal Party from 13 February 2026. She has no House of Representatives or state parliamentary service.

Evidence status

Mixed / context requiredThe claim is accurate under some definitions, periods or measures but not others. The context matters.

ABS overseas migration data show net overseas migration peaked at 555,800 in the year to September 2023, then fell to 364,820 a year later and to 305,570 in the year to June 2025. ANU demographer Alan Gamlen put the five-year average at about 260,000 a year. Gross arrivals were 568,370 in the year to June 2025, below the 607,870 recorded in the year to December 2019. The 'half a million' figure applied to one post-pandemic year, not to migration 'per year' at the time of the statement.
