Parliamentary career

Chosen by the Parliament of Victoria on 9 March 2016 under section 15 of the Constitution to fill the Senate casual vacancy created by the resignation of Senator Michael Ronaldson, then elected to the Senate for Victoria at the 2016 election and re-elected in 2019 and 2025. The Parliament of Australia records his Liberal Party service from 9 March 2016 to 9 May 2016 and from 2 July 2016. He has held no ministerial appointment; his frontbench service has been in the Shadow Ministry from June 2022. He has no House of Representatives or state parliamentary service.

Evidence status

Contradicted by evidenceThe best available evidence is inconsistent with the claim as stated.

Defence officials told Senate estimates on 26 February 2025 that the People's Liberation Army Navy's live-fire drills of 21 and 22 February 2025 took place in international waters in the Tasman Sea. Under the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea, a state's territorial sea extends 12 nautical miles from its coast and its exclusive economic zone to 200 nautical miles; the high seas lie beyond. International law experts consulted by AAP FactCheck said the drills were not in Australian waters and that live-fire exercises in Australia's territorial sea would have breached the convention.

Context

Senator Paterson's office told AAP FactCheck he had 'misspoken' and meant to say 'in our region', pointing to other occasions on which he had used that phrase. The same fact-check addressed statements by Andrew Hastie and David Coleman. The drills caused commercial aircraft to divert after a broadcast warning, which was not disputed.
